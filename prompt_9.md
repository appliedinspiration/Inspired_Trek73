An update to the calculation of the percentage chance of detonation due to damage is as follows:

The new rules completely replace the old rules. The new rules are as follows:

When a shp receives damage, whether or not it detonates due to damage is determined by the following procedure:
- If the hit is taken on a shield that is at 100% strength, the ship will not detonate due to damage.
- In the text below, "hit" refers to a hit that penetrates the shields and causes damage to ANY part of the ship.
- If a hit penetrates the shields on a ship whose engines/warp drive are at 100% strength (that is, they have taken no damage), the ship will not detonate due to damage.
- Otherwise the percentage chance of a detonation due to damage is as follows:
-    (percentage damage to the ship's engines/warp drive) / 2 + ((strength of the hit that penetrates the shields) *. 0.1)

Note that the new formula is now affected by the strength of the hit that penetrates the shields. For example, if a ship has taken 60% damage to its engines/warp drive and is hit by a weapon that penetrates the shields with a strength of 120, the percentage chance of detonation due to damage would be calculated as follows:
- Percentage chance of detonation = (60 / 2) + (120 * 0.1) = 30 + 12 = 42%
